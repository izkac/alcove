## Licence keys

Alcove is free. A licence key buys **updates** — newer versions, for the period
the key covers.

When a key expires, nothing is taken away. The version you have installed keeps
working, in full, forever. You simply stop being offered newer ones until you
renew. There is no activation server, no phone-home, and no check that can fail
and lock you out: keys are verified offline against a public key built into the
app.

A key is for one person or one organisation. Please do not post it publicly.
